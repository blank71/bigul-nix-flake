\section{Conclusion}

This tutorial gives a brief introduction to BiGUL, a putback-based
bidirectional language. It has a solid foundation, being simple to use
and powerful to be the basis for constructing various bidirectional
systems.  We are currently developing programming environment for
supporting friendly debugging of big bidirectional programs, static
checking of totality of putback functions, and dealing with
bidirectional transformation on graphs.
